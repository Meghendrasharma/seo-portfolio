The challenge

A Berlin car-assessment firm that needed to rank in many districts (Neukölln, Spandau, Marzahn …) with one website.

Initial findings

  • District pages were thin and used stock images
  • LocalBusiness schema was incomplete
  • Google Business Profile missing services, logo image and service areas

What I did

  • Launched and rewrote district location pages with real photos
  • Rewrote the LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema
  • Optimised GBP: services, service areas, images; added Google reviews to the homepage
  • Built 132 business listings and planned Quora / forum answers by keyword
  • Tracked 142 keywords by page (19 in top 10, 52 in top 20 at the last check)

Key learnings

Traffic rose sharply from February to July 2026, then dropped again from August (Ahrefs: 128 in October). Honest status: the gains were real but not yet stable; the current focus is finding why and protecting the district pages.

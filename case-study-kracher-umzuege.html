The challenge

The site lost most of its organic traffic during 2026. Five important pages dropped after a Google core update.

Initial findings

  • /leistungen/ without H1, 15 orphan pages, thank-you page in the sitemap
  • Lost pages missing important keywords (e.g. “kosten umzug berlin” on /preise/)
  • Uppercase duplicate URL and blank author/category pages (fixed)
  • Mobile PageSpeed 69, LCP 5.0 s

What I did

  • Audit of the 5 lost pages and keyword-gap fixes
  • FAQ optimisation and homepage schema
  • Competitor SERP research on Google.de for every service page
  • German → English URL plan (155 URLs) based on 20 competitor sitemaps
  • 30 branded Google Business Profile post images

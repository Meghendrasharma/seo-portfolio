The challenge

A local clearance business in a very competitive Berlin market, with thin service pages, broken internal links, several pages with missing or duplicate H1s and almost no off-page authority.

Initial findings

  • 197 internal links marked nofollow, broken contact/email links on service pages
  • Pages with missing H1 or several H1s; 16 titles longer than 60 characters
  • Low text-to-HTML ratio on 98 pages, placeholder (Lorem Ipsum) text on one page
  • No Google reviews shown on the site, missing FAQs on most service pages
  • Author archives exposing login usernames; site-wide link redirecting to an image (found in the Oct 2026 audit)

What I did

  • Logged and fixed on-page issues from Feb 2026 (H1s, broken links, GBP WhatsApp link, review widgets)
  • Rewrote 113 meta titles and descriptions
  • Built 221 backlinks: bookmarking (66), business listings (49), forums (42), social bookmarking (19), blog posts (10), Reddit & Quora
  • Prepared a 94-site citation list for local SEO
  • Built a Google Apps Script tool for programmatic location pages (drafts only, reviewed before publishing)
  • Full technical SEO report with fixes, Oct 2026

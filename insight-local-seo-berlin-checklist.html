Local SEO checklist for Berlin service businesses

What actually moved rankings on clearance, moving and car-assessment sites.

Google Business Profile

Complete services and service areas, real photos, WhatsApp/contact links, and a steady review flow shown on the website.

Location pages

One strong page per district with real images, local details and internal links to the nearest districts. Avoid copy-paste text.

Citations and listings

Consistent name, address and phone on business directories. On one project I built 132 listings; on another a 94-site citation list.

Technical basics

One H1, no broken internal links, LocalBusiness schema, fast mobile pages.

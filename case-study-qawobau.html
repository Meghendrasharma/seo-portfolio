The challenge

A new construction company website with routing problems, parameter URLs and no conversion tracking.

Initial findings

  • Broken routing and non-functional ?page parameters on product URLs
  • Login, register and cart pages were indexable
  • No canonical tags, outdated robots.txt and sitemap
  • No tracking of forms, phone or email clicks

What I did

  • Central server-side 301 redirects, removed parameter URLs, self-referencing canonicals
  • noindex,follow on login/register/password/cart pages; updated robots.txt and XML sitemap
  • Set up GA4 conversions, form, phone-click and email-click tracking
  • Rewrote title, description and H1 for “Trockenbau Berlin”, added FAQ; created new service pages
  • Built an internal-linking plan (169 links) and a keyword map (360 rows); ~200 backlinks logged

The challenge

A premium leather shop where every colour/size was its own product, splitting rankings across many near-identical URLs.

Initial findings

  • 152 product URLs for 82 products; 1,359 internal links pointing to variant URLs
  • Wrong store name in every title (“centaur-leather”), titles with line breaks
  • og:image over http, no breadcrumb or shipping schema
  • Mobile LCP 6.9 s

What I did

  • Redirect file for all 162 duplicates → main product
  • Fixed menu “#” links, typo URLs, extra H1, newsletter privacy link
  • Blog cannibalisation fix: separate keyword, title and URL per post
  • Built a daily e-commerce SEO tracker connected to Shopify data

Key learnings
